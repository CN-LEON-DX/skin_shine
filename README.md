Main functions:
  + AI Skin Analysis:
    -> Capture a selfie to receive an instant evaluation of your skin condition, accompanied by tailored product recommendations for your specific needs.

  + Live Expert Consultations:
    -> Engage in real-time chat sessions with certified skincare professionals for personalized guidance and solutions.

  + Skincare Journey Updates:
    -> Share progress photos, routines, and achievements with an in-app community to document and celebrate milestones.

  + Skincare Shop:
    > Browse and purchase products directly through the app, filtered to match your skin type, goals, and analysis results.

  + History Log:
    -> Access a timeline of past skin assessments, product trials, and expert conversations for easy reference.

  + Progress Analytics:
    -> Monitor long-term improvements via dynamic charts and metrics showcasing hydration, texture, and overall skin health trends.
